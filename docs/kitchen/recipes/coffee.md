# 🫖 Black coffee
> **Updated:** 2026-11-04

Brew and drink. No sugar, no milk. **2 cups/day, after the shower and before
morning exercise.**

## 🧹 Equipment

- Stove
- Spout pan or small pan
- Paper filter and holder
- Mug

## 🥥 Ingredients

- Ground coffee — **1¼ tbsp (~8 g) per cup**
- Water
- Paper filter

## 🧹 Method

1. Heat water until just off boiling.
2. Place a paper filter in the holder over a mug.
3. Add 1¼ tbsp ground coffee per cup.
4. Pour hot water slowly over the grounds. Let it drip through.

## 📦 Stock

- 2 cups/day × ~8 g = ~16 g/day
- ~112 g/week
- ~1.46 kg/3 months
- Buy one 500 g pack; open one at a time.

## 📌 Notes

- Coffee comes **after** the capsules and shower, not with them.
- It comes **before** the morning exercise block.
- No coffee with lunch or dinner.
- No sugar, no milk.
