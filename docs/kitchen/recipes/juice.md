# 🥤 Green juice
> **Updated:** 2026-11-04

Raw blended greens, beetroot, cucumber, lime, and ginger. **Weekdays, 2 L/day:**
1 L at lunch and 1 L at dinner. No lecithin, no sugar, no strained juice.

## 🧹 Equipment

- Blender, 2200 W target
- Knife and cutting board
- Two 1 L airtight jars
- Measuring jug

## 🥥 Ingredients — one 2 L batch

- Beetroot — ½ medium (~50–75 g)
- Couve manteiga — 3 large leaves, thick stems removed
- Spinach — 5–6 sprigs (~20–24 leaves)
- Cabbage — ⅛ small head
- Cucumber — 1 whole medium (~200 g)
- Lime — 1 whole, juiced
- Ginger — 3–4 cm
- Water — 1.2–1.4 L, added until the total is about 2 L

## 🧹 Method

1. Wash everything. Peel the cucumber if it is waxed.
2. Remove the thick couve stems. Chop the beetroot, cucumber, and cabbage.
3. Blend the vegetables with about half the water.
4. Add the spinach, couve, ginger, and lime juice. Blend until smooth.
5. Add the remaining water to reach about 2 L.
6. Pour into two 1 L jars. Fill them close to the top and close tightly.
7. Refrigerate immediately.

## 📦 Storage and schedule

- Drink **1 L with lunch** and **1 L with dinner**.
- Consume within **12 hours**; 24 hours is the outside limit.
- Keep cold. Pour a glass; do not sip from the jar.
- Shake before drinking.
- If the juice smells sour, foamy, or fizzy, discard it.

## 📌 Notes

- **No lecithin.** Choline comes from beans, liver, and sunflower seeds.
- **No sugar.** The lime is the only fruit.
- **Do not strain.** Blending keeps the fiber.
- **Start with 1 L/day** for 3–5 days if the fiber load is new.
- **Oxalates:** spinach and beet are the high-oxalate parts. If you have
  calcium-oxalate kidney stones, reduce them and use more cabbage, cucumber, and
  couve.
- **Raw cruciferous:** daily couve and cabbage are generally fine. If you have
  hypothyroidism or iodine deficiency, keep the amount moderate and use iodized
  salt.
- **Vitamin K:** very high. If you take warfarin, keep the daily amount stable.
- **Lime:** use a straw and rinse if your teeth are acid-sensitive.
