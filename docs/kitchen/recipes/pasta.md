# 🍝 Pasta + tomato sauce — optional
> **Updated:** 2026-11-04

**What this is:** an optional weekend meal. Pizza is the default; pasta replaces
a pizza meal when you want variety.

Two meals: **125 g dry pasta + 300 g tomato sauce** each. No meat, no eggs, no
oil, and no added salt.

## 🧹 Equipment

- Spout pan or pot with lid
- Knife and cutting board
- Spoon
- Plate
- Two containers

No colander: drain the pasta with the pot lid.

## 🥥 Ingredients — 2 meals

- Dry pasta — **250 g** (125 g per meal)
- Tomato sauce — **600 g** (300 g per meal)
- Onion — 1
- Garlic — 2 cloves
- Oregano — to taste
- Water

## 🧹 Method

1. Bring water to a boil; cook the pasta.
2. Warm the tomato sauce with onion, garlic, and oregano in a splash of water.
3. Drain the pasta with the pot lid; divide into two portions.
4. Top with sauce.
5. Eat one portion; refrigerate the second.

## 📦 Notes

- Pasta is **not** in the weekly baseline. Pizza is the default weekend meal.
- No added salt; the sauce and any cheese carry sodium.
- If you add cheese, count it as part of the weekend sodium.
- For a lower-sodium option, use plain crushed tomatoes instead of a seasoned
  jar sauce.
- Optional 3-month quantity if used every weekend: 3.25 kg dry pasta and about
  33 jars of 240 g tomato sauce.
