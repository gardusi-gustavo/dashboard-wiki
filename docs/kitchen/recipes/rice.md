# 🍚 Rice + salt
> **Updated:** 2026-11-04

**What this is:** the weekday rice pot — **~320 g dry white rice + 12.5 g
salt**, cooked once and divided into five daily portions.

Each daily portion is about **1 cup cooked rice**, split between lunch and
dinner. No eggs in this pot; eggs belong to the weekend pizza.

## 🧹 Equipment

- Pot or spout pan with lid
- Measuring cup
- Spoon
- Five containers

## 🥥 Ingredients — one 5-portion pot

- Dry white rice — **~320 g**
- Water — about two parts water to one part rice
- Salt — **12.5 g**

## 🧹 Method

1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear.
2. Add the rice, water, and salt to the pot.
3. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer until tender, about 15 minutes.
4. Rest covered for 5 minutes, then fluff.
5. Divide into five portions. Each is about 1 cup cooked rice.
6. Cool within 2 hours. Refrigerate 3–4 days or freeze; reheat once.

At the meal, split the daily portion: ½ cup rice at lunch and ½ cup at dinner.

## 📦 Notes

- **Salt:** 12.5 g in the pot = 2.5 g/day across five portions. The bean/liver
  pot carries the other 2.5 g/day.
- **No eggs, no oil, no extra seasoning.** The beans carry the spices.
- Rice is a vehicle for the beans and juice, not a separate protein source.
- Reheat until steaming hot. Do not leave cooked rice at room temperature.
