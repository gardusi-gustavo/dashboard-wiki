# 🥣 Black beans + liver
> **Updated:** 2026-11-04

**What this is:** the weekday bean/liver pot — **1 kg dry black beans + 200 g
raw beef liver**, pressure-cooked once and divided into five portions.

Each daily portion is about **2.5 cups cooked beans + 40 g liver**, split
between lunch and dinner. The rice is separate.

## 🧹 Equipment

- Pressure cooker
- Knife and cutting board
- Spoon
- Five 1 L containers

## 🥥 Ingredients — one 5-portion pot

- Dry black beans — **1 kg**
- Beef liver, raw — **200 g**
- Onion — 1
- Garlic — 2 cloves
- Salt — **12.5 g**
- Black pepper — 5 g
- Garlic powder — 5 g
- Turmeric — 5 g
- Smoked paprika — 5 g
- Chimichurri — 5 g
- Water — enough to cover by about 3 cm

## 🧹 Method

1. Rinse the beans and soak overnight if convenient. Drain.
2. Add the beans, onion, garlic, salt, spices, and water to the pressure cooker.
3. Pressure-cook until the beans are very soft, 25–40 minutes.
4. Add the liver for the last 10–15 minutes, or cook it separately and stir it
   in after the pressure drops. Do not overcook it into rubber.
5. Release the pressure naturally. Shred or dice the liver and stir it through
   the beans.
6. Divide into five containers. Each gets about 2.5 cups beans + 40 g liver.
7. Cool within 2 hours. Refrigerate 3–4 days or freeze; reheat once.

## 📦 Notes

- **Salt:** 12.5 g in the pot = 2.5 g/day across five portions. The rice pot
  carries the other 2.5 g/day.
- **Liver:** 200 g/week is the choline, B12, iron, and vitamin A source.
- **No pork, no chicken, no beef muscle meat.** Ham is weekend pizza only.
- **No oil.**
- **No separate raw blend.** The 2 L green juice carries the vegetables.
- Serve half the daily beans/liver at lunch and half at dinner.
