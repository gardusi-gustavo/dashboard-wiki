# 🍕 Portuguese pizza
> **Updated:** 2026-11-04

**What this is:** one 35 cm, 8-slice Portuguese pizza for the weekend. Two
slices at Saturday lunch and dinner, and two at Sunday lunch and dinner.

No peas, no olives, no oil, no sugar, and no added salt.

## 🧹 Equipment

- Baking tray or pizza pan
- Oven
- Pot for boiling eggs
- Knife and cutting board
- Spoon

## 🥥 Ingredients — one 35 cm pizza

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| --- | ---: |
| Pre-made pizza base | 1 large, 8 slices |
| Crushed tomatoes / passata | 300 g |
| Chopped ham | 250 g |
| Eggs | 8 |
| Onion | 1 medium |
| Mozzarella | 250 g |
| Oregano | 1–2 tsp |

Check the base label for added oil or sugar. If it has either, that is the only
hidden source.

## 🧹 Method

1. Boil the **8 eggs** for 9–10 minutes. Cool in cold water, peel, and chop.
2. Preheat the oven to **250 °C** for raw dough, or **220 °C** for a pre-baked
   base. Follow the base package if it differs.
3. Spread the **300 g tomato sauce** over the base, leaving a 1–2 cm border.
4. Add the **250 g chopped ham**.
5. Add the **8 chopped eggs** evenly.
6. Add the **1 thinly sliced onion**.
7. Cover with **250 g mozzarella**.
8. Sprinkle **1–2 tsp oregano**. Do not add salt.
9. Bake 10–12 minutes, until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.
10. Rest 5 minutes. Slice into 8 pieces.

Layer order:

```text
base -> tomato sauce -> ham -> chopped eggs -> onion -> mozzarella -> oregano
```

## 🍽️ Weekend schedule

| Meal | Slices |
| --- | ---: |
| Saturday lunch | 2 |
| Saturday dinner | 2 |
| Sunday lunch | 2 |
| Sunday dinner | 2 |

The whole pizza is eaten across the weekend. No pasta unless it replaces one of
these meals.

## 📊 Nutrition

| Metric | Whole pizza | Per slice |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Calories | ~2,600–2,800 | ~325–350 |
| Protein | ~165–175 g | ~21 g |
| Choline | ~1,200 mg | ~150 mg |
| Sodium | ~4,000–7,000 mg | ~500–900 mg |

Sodium depends mostly on the ham, mozzarella, sauce, and base. Use plain
crushed tomatoes and lower-sodium ham/cheese when possible.

## 📦 Notes

- **No added salt.** The ingredients carry enough sodium.
- **No oil.** Do not brush the crust.
- **No sugar.** Do not add sugar to the sauce.
- **No peas, no olives.**
- Leftovers: refrigerate within 2 hours; eat within 3 days; reheat at 180 °C or
  in a covered pan.
- The weekend day is roughly 1,600–1,700 kcal with the lean breakfast.
