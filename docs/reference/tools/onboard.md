# 🏠 Onboard
> **Updated:** 2026-11-02

**What this is:** the full kit to buy when arriving at a new home — assume the unit has nothing, inventory first, and buy only what is missing. Buy for **this stay**, not the next one; at move-out, donate or leave what does not fit the backpack.

## 🧭 Principle

- **Assume nothing.** Inventory the unit first; cross off what is present.
- Furnished Airbnb: rely on furniture and appliances, not consumables or tools.
- **Buy cheap, use hard, donate/leave.** Do not pay for durability or optimize for carrying leftovers.
- One backpack is the mobility limit. Carry only what is compact or expensive to replace.
- **Priority rule:** **high** = must buy; **medium** = buy if the budget allows; **low** = apprentice/precision items; buy later.
- **Return rule:** low price + real use = buy. High price + low importance = skip.
- **Unit-provided checks:** fridge, stove/oven, microwave. A missing fridge makes a cooking stay unsuitable; a missing stove means a cheap portable hot plate.

## 🍳 Kitchen

The kitchen is a pressure cooker plus the bare setting for one person. The unit provides the fridge, and the stove when present. Everything else is bought by priority.

### Stay length

| Stay | Buy |
| --- | --- |
| **1–3 nights** | Nothing durable; use the unit |
| **1–2 weeks** | High only, and only if cooking |
| **1–3 months** | High + medium that earns its value; expand containers if the routine sticks |
| **3+ months** | High + medium; low if learning; six 1 kg seasoning packs |

## 🥡 Batch storage

- **Start with 3 × 1 L containers.** Add **2 × 1 L**, then **5 × 600 ml**, to reach the full 10-container system.
- Full system: **5 × 1 L lunch + 5 × 600 ml dinner**; 2 containers/day.
- Sunday: cook, cool within 2 hours, portion, label Mon–Fri.
- Fridge Mon–Wed; freeze Thu–Fri; move the next day’s containers to the fridge the night before.
- Keep a “today” bin in the fridge.
- Reheat once, steaming hot; add a splash of water to beans/rice.
- For a 3+ month stay, expand early if batch cooking actually sticks. For a short stay, 3 containers are enough.

## 🎒 Personal carry-over

Small, expensive-to-replace items travel between stays:

- toothbrush
- toothpaste
- solid soap
- shampoo
- deodorant
- one sealed 10 g roach gel syringe
- gloves
- small flashlight

Check transport rules for the gel; do not carry an opened or leaking syringe.
Buy bulky items — water filter, refills, frying pan — locally.

## 🗑️ Move-out

Use this order:

1. **Donate** to a neighbor, cleaner, owner, or local person who can use it.
2. **Leave it in the unit** if it is useful and the owner/cleaner can place it.
3. **Bin/recycle** only if it is broken, unsafe, or unwanted.

Empty and dry the tools; do not deep-clean what is going to donation. Carry only the backpack; leave bulky cheap items. Do not optimize purchases for carrying leftovers to the next stay.
