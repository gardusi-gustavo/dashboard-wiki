# 🧂 Seasoning stock
> **Updated:** 2026-11-02

**What this is:** kitchen stock, not a recipe. Buy six seasonings in 1 kg chunks, use them as needed, and donate or leave the rest at move-out.

**Standard:** the bean/liver pot carries the spices; the rice pot carries only
salt. Pizza uses oregano and no added salt.

## 🥄 Batch method

| Meal | Portions | Salt | Each of the five salt-free seasonings | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Weekday beans + liver | 5 | 12.5 g | 5 g each | 37.5 g |
| Weekday rice | 5 | 12.5 g | — | 12.5 g |
| Weekend pizza | 1 | 0 g | oregano only | — |

- Use the **kitchen scale** if available (1 g precision, tare).
- No scale is fine for the spices; weigh the salt.
- For the 5-portion bean pot, weigh **12.5 g salt + 5 g of each spice**.
- For the 5-portion rice pot, weigh **12.5 g salt only**.

## 🧂 Salt

- **5 g added salt/day** = 2.5 g from beans + 2.5 g from rice.
- **25 g/week**.
- Sodium from added salt is ~2,000 mg/day; food sodium brings weekdays to
  ~2,200 mg.
- Weekends: no added salt; pizza ingredients carry the sodium.

## 📦 Stock

| Seasoning | Pack | Target price | Conservative life |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Salt | 1 kg | — | ~6 months |
| Black pepper | 1 kg | — | ~6 months |
| Garlic powder | 1 kg | — | ~6 months |
| Turmeric | 1 kg | — | ~6 months |
| Smoked paprika | 1 kg | — | ~6 months |
| Chimichurri | 1 kg | — | ~6 months |
| **Total** | **6 kg** | **—** | **~6 months** |

At **1 g per portion** and 12 portions/week, a 1 kg pack can last longer than six months. Use **six months as the conservative budget cycle**, not a hard expiry.

## 🧳 Stay rule

| Stay | Buy |
| --- | --- |
| **<1 month** | Small packs, or use what is already there |
| **1–3 months** | 1 kg packs only if cheap and you will use most of them |
| **3+ months** | Buy the six 1 kg packs, — total |

This is a **one-time seasoning stock**, separate from the 3-month pantry cycle. It is worth buying early for a 3+ month stay because the packs keep and the per-use cost is low.

## 🗑️ Donation / leave-behind

Do not plan around carrying six half-used packs. Use what you need, then:

1. **Donate** to a neighbor, cleaner, owner, or local person.
2. **Leave it in the unit** if the owner/cleaner can place it.
3. **Bin/recycle** only if it is spoiled or unwanted.

## 📝 Notes

- No separate onion powder: garlic powder plus salt covers the base.
- Chimichurri already carries herbs and white pepper; check its salt.
- Pizza is the default weekend meal; no pizza seasoning stock beyond oregano.
- Pasta is optional.
- Adjust the spice amounts as taste develops; keep salt at 5 g/day.
