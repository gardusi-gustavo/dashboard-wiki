## 📦 Notes

- These are **replacement costs**, not a monthly shopping list.
- Keep the receipt and purchase date; the expected life is a planning estimate.
- A cheap tool that fails early is not cheaper than a mid-range tool that lasts.
- Do not optimize for carrying leftovers; donate or leave bulky items at
  move-out.
