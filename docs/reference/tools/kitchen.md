# 🍳 Kitchen tools — blender, pressure cooker, spout pan
> **Updated:** 2026-11-02

**What this is:** how to use the three kitchen tools that carry the diet. The
buy list lives in `docs/reference/tools/onboard.md`; prices and expected life
live in `docs/reference/tools/costs.md`.

## 🥤 Blender

### Safety

- Never put hands or a spatula into the jar while it is plugged in.
- Add ingredients **through the lid hole** while it is running.
- Unplug before touching the blades or cleaning around them.
- Do not overfill; leave headspace.

### Method

1. Chop ingredients small enough to fit through the lid opening — roughly
   2–3 cm pieces.
2. Remove tough stems and hard seeds.
3. Start with **little water** — enough to keep the blades moving, not to fill
   the jar.
4. Turn on, then add pieces slowly through the lid hole.
5. A few seconds usually chops everything to drinkable size.
6. Add more water at the end to reach the consistency you want.
7. If it stalls: stop, unplug, shake or add water. Do not use hands.

### Clean

- Rinse immediately; do not submerge the motor base.

## 🍲 Pressure cooker

### Before

- Check the **silicone gasket** and safety valve; the lid must seal tight.
- Fill no more than **⅔**; beans/foaming food no more than **½**.
- Use enough water; never let it run dry.

### Heat

- Start high to reach pressure.
- Then **low fire for longer** — low-and-slow is more successful than high heat.
- It is fine to use extra water; after pressure drops, simmer it off.

### After

- Turn the stove off.
- **Wait naturally** for pressure to stop — a few minutes.
- Do not accelerate with cold water unless the manufacturer allows it.
- Never force the lid.
- The cooker cannot “turn off instantly”; respect the cool-down.

### Framing

- The pressure cooker is the **bazooka** of the kitchen: powerful, fast for
  beans, and not a quick-toggle appliance.

## 🫕 Spout pan — *panela com bico / papeiro*

### Uses

- Boiling water
- Rice, pasta
- Sauces, broths, melting ingredients
- Clean pouring without mess — the spout is the point.

### Method

- Use the spout side away from your body.
- Use a lid when bringing water to a boil.
- Pour slowly; the spout controls the stream.
- Use mitts; steam burns.

### Role

- The pressure cooker is the bazooka for beans and bulk.
- The spout pan is the precise, small-batch tool for water, rice, sauces, and
  pouring.
