# 🌀 Flows — how a move spreads

**What this is:** labels for the channel that started or amplified a move — public social, retail
broker flow, options, media, institutional, company disclosure, political/legal, environmental, and
hidden private groups — plus what a retail observer can actually see. **Illustrative. Not financial
advice.**

## 🔍 What is visible

- **Short interest and days to cover:** public, but lagged.
- **Borrow fee:** visible through the broker, but can change quickly.
- **Free float:** public; low float makes a squeeze easier.
- **ADTV and spread:** visible; low liquidity makes manipulation easier.
- **Options open interest:** public; large call OI can amplify a move.
- **Volume and price:** visible in real time, but after the move has started.
- **News:** visible, but not always true.
- **Social mentions:** visible, but noisy and gameable.

## 🕳️ What stays hidden

- **Private WhatsApp/Telegram/Discord groups:** coordination leaves little public trace.
- **Dark pools and off-exchange trades:** not on the visible tape.
- **Hidden ownership and strategic stakes:** disclosed only when required.
- **Dealer hedging:** visible only through aggregate OI, not the full book.
- **Intent:** even public flow does not tell you when the group will sell.

By the time the volume and price confirm the flow, the move may be halfway done. You cannot reliably
detect a private group; you can only avoid the setups that make manipulation easy.

## 🧪 Cases

### 🎮 GME

- **Flow:** public social + retail broker + options.
- **Visible early:** high short interest/float, days to cover, call OI.
- **Not visible:** the Reddit coordination, the timing, and the size.
- **Lesson:** the setup was public; the exact path was not.

### 🚗 VW 2008

- **Flow:** institutional/strategic; Porsche’s stake.
- **Visible:** borrow, price, and disclosures — late.
- **Not visible:** the intent until the announcement.
- **Lesson:** a corner can be built quietly; borrow data is a clue, not a warning system.

### 🛒 AMER3

- **Flow:** company disclosure + accounting.
- **Visible early:** FCF conversion, DSO/DPO, store traffic, competitors.
- **Not visible:** the exact disclosure date.
- **Lesson:** the accounting signals were early; the headline was late.

### 🛢️ PETR4

- **Flow:** political/legal.
- **Visible:** the headline at the moment it happens.
- **Not visible:** the timing.
- **Lesson:** size for political gap risk; a low P/E is not protection.

## ⚠️ Limits

- Seeing the setup is not the same as knowing the timing.
- Private groups can coordinate without public trace.
- Short interest and borrow data are lagged.
- News can be false; verify the source.
- Volume can be wash trading or a single block.
- A squeeze can happen to a good company or a bad one.
- Fundamentals do not predict a squeeze; positioning does not predict a long-term return.

## ✅ Defense

1. **Liquidity gate first.** Low float + low ADTV = skip.
2. **Avoid crowded shorts.** High short interest + high borrow + low float is a squeeze setup.
3. **Keep a collateral buffer.** 120% is a minimum, not a target.
4. **Use a protective call** if the short is large or hard to borrow.
5. **Do not chase a move because it is spreading.** The flow is not a thesis.
6. **Verify the news.** A rumour is not a filing.
7. **Size for the gap.** The stop may not fill.
