# 🧾 Brazil stock tax rules
> **Updated:** 2026-11-02

**What this is:** generic B3/CPF capital-gains rules. Not tax advice; confirm
current Receita Federal, B3, and broker rules.

## 📜 Basics

- Capital gains on variable income are taxed when you sell. Buying alone is not
  a taxable event.
- Losses offset gains in the same category.
- Gains/losses are grouped by month.

## 📊 Rates

| Asset | Rate | R$20k/month stock exemption |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Brazilian stocks | 15% | yes |
| ETFs | 15% | no |
| BDRs | 15% | no |
| FIIs | 20% | no |

## 🛡️ Stock exemption

Selling **stocks** for **R$20,000 or less total in a month** is exempt: no DARF,
and no profit is computed for that month.

ETFs, BDRs, and FIIs have no such exemption — any gain is taxable regardless of
the month's total.

## 💰 Above R$20k

- Stock gains are taxed at **15%** once monthly stock sales exceed R$20k.
- DARF código **6015**.
- Due by the **last business day of the following month**.
- If the exact amount is uncertain, round up; overpayment can be recovered or
  compensated in the next IRPF or via PER/DCOMP.

## 🧮 Losses

- Offset gains in the same category.
- A loss month pays no DARF.
- Keep the loss record for future months.
