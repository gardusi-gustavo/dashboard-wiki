# 📊 Net-worth routine
> **Updated:** 2026-11-02

**What this is:** generic monthly net-worth review and reconciliation. Actual
balances, accounts, and personal amounts stay in the private ledger.

## 🧭 Steps

1. Update fixed-income balances from each app/statement.
2. Check daily float and expense pools against the expense summary.
3. Reconcile against statements.
4. Recompute quick metrics and net monthly.
5. Update notes if burn or policy changed.

## 💰 Account separation

- One receiving/honeypot account for income.
- Separate spending pools for daily float, backup, and cash.
- Keep physical and virtual cards with clear roles.
- Expense pools are daily float, not net worth; top up from reserve when low.
- Keep reserve and risk capital separate from spending.

## 🔎 Review checklist

- Fixed-income balances match the apps.
- Pool float is within caps.
- Monthly net stays ≥ 0.
- Stock orders, closed cycles, and DARF receipts are current.
- Losses carried forward; taxes paid by the deadline.
- Overpayment recovered/compensated at year-end.
