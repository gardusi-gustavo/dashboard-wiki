# 🧺 Resource management — cashflow, reserve, and allocation

**What this is:** the money-management layer around the investment pages — what to do before picking
an instrument, how much cash to keep, how to handle debt and insurance, and how to size risk.
**Brazil, rules as of 2026 — verify current rules. Not financial advice.**

---

## 💵 Cashflow

- **Know the monthly number:** income minus fixed costs minus variable costs.
- **Savings rate** matters more than the return on a small portfolio at the start.
- **Separate accounts** for spending, reserve, and investments reduce accidental spending.
- **Annual costs** — taxes, insurance, travel, maintenance — are monthly costs divided by twelve.

## 🛟 Reserve

<!-- begin table -->
| Situation | Reserve | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stable salary | 3–6 months | Daily-liquidity CDB / Tesouro Selic |
| Variable income | 6–12 months | Same, plus a cash buffer |
| Dependents | 6–12 months | Same, sized to the household |
| Business owner | 6–12 months | Same, plus working capital |
<!-- end table -->

The reserve is not an investment. It is the thing that prevents forced selling at the worst time.

## 💳 Debt

- **High-interest debt first:** revolving credit and card debt usually cost more than any safe
  investment returns.
- **Compare after tax:** a 15% investment gain taxed at 15% is not 15% net; debt interest is usually
  certain.
- **Do not invest borrowed money** unless the operation is explicitly a leveraged strategy with a
  stop and a size limit.

## 🛡️ Insurance

- Health, life, and disability cover protect the plan from a single event.
- Insurance is not an investment; compare the premium to the size of the loss it removes.
- Read exclusions before relying on the payout.

## 🧺 Allocation

<!-- begin table -->
| Bucket | Job | Instruments |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Liquidity** | Emergency and near-term spend | CDB daily, Tesouro Selic |
| **Safety** | Capital preservation | Tesouro, short CDB ladder |
| **Growth** | Long-term real return | Long-only stocks, IPCA+ |
| **Speculation** | Bounded risk | Short selling, concentrated positions |
<!-- end table -->

- **Time horizon** decides the bucket, not the headline yield.
- **Risk capacity** is financial; **risk tolerance** is emotional. Size to the lower one.
- **Rebalance** annually or when a bucket drifts beyond its band.
- **Concentration** is a choice: a single stock can be a thesis, but it is not a reserve.
- **Keep a margin buffer:** do not allocate the full reserve into Tesouro or another locked asset
  before a leveraged or short operation. Settlement and haircuts can leave a same-day gap.

## ✅ Decision checklist

1. What is the goal and the date?
2. Can this money be locked, or does it need liquidity?
3. What is the tax and fee cost of the exit?
4. Who is the issuer, and what happens if it fails?
5. Is this collateral, reserve, or risk capital?
6. What is the invalidation point?
7. What is the position size if it halves?

## ❓ Open questions

- What is the real monthly cost of the current routine?
- How many months of reserve are actually needed for this income pattern?
- Which bucket is currently overfunded?
