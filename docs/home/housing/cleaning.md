# 🧹 Cleaning

**What this is:** the cleaning cadence for a two-bedroom, living room, kitchen,
laundry, and bathroom unit. Keep it small enough to repeat.

## ☀️ Daily

- dishes and cooking tools
- trash out, especially food waste
- wipe counters and stovetop
- dry the sink and shower
- sweep the kitchen floor

## 📅 Weekly

- clean kitchen surfaces and stovetop
- clean bathroom sink, toilet, and shower
- laundry: clothes, towels, bed linen
- floors in kitchen, bathroom, and living room
- inspect bait points
- check the closed bedroom door gap and vents

## 🗓️ Monthly

- clean under the stove and fridge edges without disconnecting anything
- clean drains and replace or clear traps
- pantry rotation and expired food check
- wipe cabinet fronts and handles
- clean microwave and small appliances
- dust vents and baseboards
- check the laundry hose area for leaks

## 🧼 Quarterly

- deep clean oven and hood filter
- wash curtains and mattress cover
- clean windows and tracks
- declutter cardboard and paper storage
- review supplies and reorder
- re-inspect all pest points

## 🧯 Safety

- never mix cleaning products
- ventilate when using strong products
- keep products away from food and pets
- do not move gas appliances
- ask the host before any structural change
