# 🍳 Kitchen
> **Updated:** 2026-11-02

**What this is:** food storage and kitchen habits that keep pests out and waste
down. Quantities live in [pantry](../consumables/pantry.md) and
[buy list](../consumables/buy-list.md).

## 🥫 Storage

- airtight containers for rice, beans, flour, pasta, and seeds
- no open bags in cabinets
- fruit and vegetables checked daily
- leftovers cooled within two hours and labeled
- freezer bags flattened for quick freezing

## 🔄 Rotation

- first in, first out
- pantry capped at three months
- weekly check of perishables
- monthly check of opened packages
- discard swollen, leaking, or expired items

## 🧽 Grease and crumbs

- wipe the stovetop after every cooking session
- clean the hood filter monthly
- pull the stove only if the host allows it
- sweep under the table and counters weekly
- do not leave dirty dishes overnight

## 🗑️ Trash

- food waste out daily
- recycling rinsed and drained
- bin washed weekly
- no cardboard storage in dark cabinets
- confirm collection day with the host

## 🚰 Drains

- keep strainers in sinks
- close floor drains when not in use
- pour hot water through the kitchen drain weekly
- report slow drains to the host
- do not use harsh drain chemicals preemptively

## 🪳 Pest prevention

- no food left out
- no open cereal or pet food
- check the back of cabinets monthly
- log sightings in [sightings.md](sightings.md)
