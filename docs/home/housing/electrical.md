# ⚡ Electrical — voltage, outlets, and high-power appliances

**What this is:** the pre-purchase check for anything that draws real power —
blender, hot plate, kettle, heater, air conditioner, washer, shower. Brazil uses
127 V and 220 V; the unit decides, not the city.

## Rule

- Check the outlet before buying. A 127 V appliance on 220 V is destroyed; a
  220 V appliance on 127 V does not deliver.
- “110 V” in Brazil usually means **127 V nominal**. Treat 110/127 as the same
  low-voltage class, but verify.
- Most outlets in a unit share one voltage. One or two circuits may differ:
  laundry, electric shower, air conditioner, oven/cooktop, or a dedicated
  kitchen/workshop circuit.
- City pattern is a prior, not a fact: **São Paulo common 127 V; Minas Gerais
  common 220 V**. The building and the unit can differ.

## How to check

1. Ask the landlord/host for the unit voltage and any 220 V circuits.
2. Read the appliance label and the outlet/breaker labels.
3. Use a socket tester or multimeter on the actual outlet.
4. Photograph the outlet/label before buying.
5. Check the plug: NBR 14136 10 A vs 20 A; no adapters for high-power appliances.
6. Check the breaker and wire gauge. A 2200 W load at 127 V draws ~17 A; at
   220 V it draws ~10 A.
7. Buy with a return policy; test on arrival.

## High-power math

| Appliance | Power | Current at 127 V | Current at 220 V |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Oster 1400 Full | 1400 W | ~11 A | — |
| Arno Power Max 2200 | 2200 W | — | ~10 A |
| Hot plate | 1500–2000 W | ~12–16 A | ~7–9 A |
| Electric shower | 5500 W | ~43 A | ~25 A |

A 10 A outlet is marginal for a 1400 W blender at 127 V. Confirm a 20 A outlet
and breaker before buying; otherwise use a lower-power appliance or another
circuit.

## Blender picks

| Voltage | Model | Power | Jar | Price |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 127 V / “110 V” | Oster 1400 Full | 1400 W | 3.2 L | ~R$180–230 |
| 220 V | Arno Power Max 2200 (LN66) | 2200 W | 3.1 L | ~R$210–230 |

Both are under R$300; the working figure is **~R$200**. Match the outlet; do not
buy across voltages.
