# 🪳 Pests
> **Updated:** 2026-11-02

**What this is:** how to inspect for cockroaches, treat them safely, and decide
when the host or a professional must take over. Log every sighting in
[sightings.md](sightings.md).

## 🧭 Principles

- Remove food, water, and harborage before treating.
- The exterior is infinite. The goal is a dead-end interior, not a dead street.
- Gel bait and enclosed stations beat sprays, foggers, and spiders.
- Small amounts, in cracks, not large blobs on open surfaces.
- Inspect and renew every 6–7 weeks; replace dried, dirty, or consumed bait.
- Do not spray over bait or clean it with strong products.
- In a rented unit, tell the host before non-trivial treatment.

## 🎯 Defense model

Treat the house as concentric zones:

| Zone | Meaning | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outside | Street, drains, soil, neighbouring lots | Uncontrollable. Do not bait. |
| Rim | Windows, doors, sink and floor drains | Seal what you can. No gel here. |
| Outer ring | Baseboards and walls near entries | Sparse checkpoints only. |
| Inner ring | Hallways and transitions | A few checkpoints. |
| Core | Kitchen, fridge, stove, sink, bathroom, laundry | Highest density; the kill zone. |

The colony is the target. A scout at the rim is not worth attracting inward.

## 🗺️ First-day map

1. Walk the unit with a flashlight.
2. Draw or list every window, door, sink, floor drain, fridge, stove, bathroom, laundry, and closed bedroom.
3. Mark **entry points** and **harbourage points** separately.
4. Apply the first gel pass to the core and interior baseboards, not the rim.
5. Label each point with the date.
6. Photograph the map for the next renewal and the next stay.

## 🔎 Identify

| Sign | Likely |
| --- | --- |
| Small nymphs, daytime activity, indoor breeding | German cockroach |
| Large adult, drains, laundry, bathroom, occasional | American or oriental cockroach |
| Egg cases, droppings, grease marks | Established harborage |

A single slow adult can mean poison, age, or a crowded harborage. Log it; do not
conclude from one sighting.

For the species split and the harbourage campaign, see
[pest-species.md](pest-species.md).

## 🗺️ Inspection map

### Kitchen

- under and behind the stove, without moving gas connections
- behind and under the fridge
- sink cabinet, pipe entries, dishwasher hose
- lower cabinet hinges and corners
- microwave and small appliances
- trash bin and recycling
- cardboard boxes and bags

### Laundry

- sink and drain
- washer hoses and floor drain
- shelves, baskets, and dark corners

### Bathroom

- under the sink, pipe entries
- floor drain and shower trap
- toilet base
- cabinet and mirror edges

### Living room and bedrooms

- baseboards
- sofa and bed frames
- nightstands and wardrobes
- electronics and cable gaps
- closed bedroom: door gap, vents, outlets, wardrobe

### Entry

- door gap and threshold
- packages and bags
- shoes and storage

## 🧪 Treatment

### Gel bait

- Tiny drops, about a grain of rice.
- Put them on the **top edge of the baseboard**, not on the floor. This keeps
  them out of the cleaning path and at roach travel height.
- Space every 1–2 m on high-traffic walls; use 50 cm only in a heavy-risk zone.
- Core placements: under the sink and pipe entries, behind and under the
  fridge, the sides and rear of the stove, lower cabinet corners, bathroom and
  laundry corners, around floor drains but not inside standing water.
- Do **not** put gel on window sills, door thresholds, exterior frames, or
  beside an open entry. The bait is a food attractant; at the rim it can pull
  more roaches inward.
- Avoid direct heat, food surfaces, and surfaces that are cleaned daily.
- Do not put gel inside the oven or on a hot stove.
- Do not bait inside a drain, sink, or standing water; keep drain covers and
  strainers in place.
- Do not apply where pets or children can reach.
- Renew every 6–7 weeks; remove dried or dirty bait before reapplying.
- First pass may use up to four 10 g syringes in a full house, within the
  product label. More is not better; over-application can be ignored or spread.

### Enclosed stations

- Place along baseboards and under appliances.
- Good for long duration and open floors.
- Keep away from food preparation surfaces.
- Replace according to the label or when dirty.

### Naming ideas

Use these as examples only; verify local registration, label, and availability:

- gel bait: K-Othrine Gel, Straik Gel, Advion Gel, Goliath Gel
- stations: Raid Iscas, Mortein Pro, Combat
- generic: gel bait syringe, enclosed bait station, crack-and-crevice applicator

## ⏱️ What to expect

| Time | Expectation |
| --- | --- |
| Day 0 | Full first-pass map; no visible change |
| Days 1–3 | First dead or slow roaches |
| Week 1 | Peak chaos: more sightings, disoriented roaches, dead ones near bait |
| Week 2 | New arrivals die faster; activity concentrates around bait |
| Weeks 3–6 | Decline; replace consumed bait |
| Week 7 | Renew all points |
| Ongoing | Rain, open windows, doors, and drains can always bring a new scout |

## 🔁 Renewal

- Inspect and renew every **6–7 weeks**.
- Remove dried, dirty, or consumed bait; clean around it without spraying over it.
- Reapply grain-sized dots to the same core map and any new harbourage.
- Log the date and any sightings in [sightings.md](sightings.md).
- If nymphs, daytime activity, or repeated sightings appear, escalate instead
  of only renewing.

Log the renewal date in a private task list.

## 🎒 Carry-over

- One sealed 10 g gel syringe travels between stays.
- Add gloves and a small flashlight.
- Check local transport rules; do not carry an opened or leaking syringe.
- Buy bulky items — water filter, refills, frying pan — locally.

## 🚫 Do not

- fog the unit as a first step
- spray over bait
- over-apply gel
- put gel inside an oven or on a hot stove
- put gel on window sills, door thresholds, or exterior frames
- bait a drain or an open entry
- rely on spiders or house centipedes
- seal or move gas appliances yourself in a rental
- use a product outside its label

## 🚨 Escalate

Tell the host or call a professional when:

- nymphs are seen
- sightings happen in daylight
- the same room produces repeated sightings
- bait is ignored for two cycles
- the closed bedroom, laundry drain, or bathroom drain shows activity
- the infestation crosses units
