# 🔧 Maintenance

**What this is:** renter-safe checks that prevent damage and pests. Do not
disconnect gas, open walls, or change fixtures without the host.

## Monthly

- test smoke detector
- check under sinks for leaks
- clean sink and shower drains
- inspect washer hoses
- vacuum fridge coils if accessible
- check window seals and condensation
- inspect bait points and replace if needed

## Quarterly

- clean hood filter
- clean oven
- wash curtains and mattress cover
- check furniture fixings
- review emergency contacts and host details
- check the closed bedroom for damp or pests

## Seasonal

- windows and tracks
- wardrobe and stored clothing
- mattress rotation
- donation or disposal of unused items
- check stay end date and renewal

## Report to the host

- persistent damp or mold
- slow drains
- electrical faults — see [electrical.md](electrical.md)
- gas smell
- pest activity that survives two bait cycles
- broken locks or windows
