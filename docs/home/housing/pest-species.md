# 🪳 Pest species and harbourage

**What this is:** how to tell the two large cockroaches apart, and how to
eradicate one that has moved in rather than merely passed through. Zone rules
and bait placement stay in [pests.md](pests.md); dated observations go in
[sightings.md](sightings.md).

## 🧬 Two species, two campaigns

| | *Periplaneta americana* | *Blatta orientalis* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Names | barata americana, barata de esgoto | barata oriental |
| Flight | yes, in warm humid weather; rarely indoors | no; the female is wingless |
| Speed | fast, hard to catch | slow, easy to catch |
| Colour | reddish brown, pale ring on the pronotum | uniform dark brown to black |
| Source | sewer main, grease trap, outdoor mulch | damp voids, drains, ground floor |
| Breeding | off site | can breed indoors |
| Goal | reduce arrivals, kill intruders | treat the harbourage, then close it |
| Realistic | never zero | zero over weeks |

The campaigns are not interchangeable. Bait on a transient is wasted; contact
spray on a nursery does nothing.

## 🔎 Identify

| Sign | Likely |
| --- | --- |
| Wingless, adult sized, slow, uniform dark | *B. orientalis* female |
| Wingless, smaller, reddish with pale margins | *P. americana* nymph |
| Full wings, reddish, pale ring on the pronotum | *P. americana* adult |
| Two dark stripes on the pronotum | *B. germanica*, a different campaign |

Nymphs confound the call. Decide from a caught specimen, photographed from
above and side on. Do not conclude from one sighting, and do not inherit a
species name from a conversation as if it were a fact.

## 🧪 The decisive test

Cut a glue board to fit and slide it flat into the suspected void, resting on
the surface the roaches use. Read it after 7 days.

- **Nymphs present:** a nursery behind that surface. Use the sequence below.
- **Adults only:** a doorway. Use the checkpoints in [pests.md](pests.md).
- **Empty:** the route is elsewhere. Move the board before treating.

The board is a meter, not a treatment. Keep one in place after treatment, or
there is no way to know whether the work succeeded.

## 🧹 Eradication sequence

Suppression is poison plus time. Eradication is removal, lethal surfaces, a
reproduction stop, and closure, in that order.

1. **Remove.** Vacuum the void and its lip, then bin the bag outside. This is
   the only step that removes egg cases, frass, and cast skins; no insecticide
   kills an ootheca.
2. **Coat.** Apply a residual film to the interior resting surfaces, not glue
   and not blobs. A film persists for weeks and kills what lives against it,
   including individuals that never forage.
3. **Stop reproduction.** An IGR prevents the next generation instead of
   killing the current one. Optional where it is unavailable; without it the
   decline is slower, not impossible.
4. **Close.** Seal the void only after steps 1 to 3 have run for two to three
   weeks. Sealing a treated harbourage buries it; sealing an untreated one
   moves the population to another exit.

Bait belongs in the sequence as the transfer layer for foragers. Bait alone is
suppression.

## 🎯 Which zone is which

Applying the [pests.md](pests.md) zones to a harbourage:

- A void above a ceiling, or a cavity behind a fitted panel, is **core**: it is
  where a colony would live. Bait at its lip is a core placement.
- A window, door, or exterior frame is **rim**: no gel. Clean the channels, fit
  mesh, seal the gap, and trap.
- A floor drain is **rim**: cover it, flush the water seal, no bait inside.

The question to ask is whether the place is where they *live* or where they
*arrive*. Living is core. Arriving is rim.

## ⏱️ What to expect

| Time | Expectation |
| --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | more sightings, disoriented roaches |
| Week 1 | first counts on the board |
| Weeks 2–3 | counts fall; nymphs are the signal that matters |
| Weeks 3–4 | seal the void only if counts are near zero |
| After sealing | one board stays in place as a check |
| Rain, garden work, building work | a 2–4 day wave is expected, not a failure |

## 🚫 Do not

- treat a void before measuring it with a board, unless the spend is blind
- seal before the sequence has run
- dust a wet void; it cakes and cannot be verified
- bait a window, threshold, drain, or exterior frame
- fog, or broadcast spray a repellent
- spray a pyrethroid where cats walk; they groom and ingest it
- assume one active will keep working; rotate if bait is ignored for a cycle
- claim a species or a nest from a single sighting

## 🚨 Escalate

Tell the host or call a professional when nymphs appear, sightings happen in
daylight, a smell persists, egg cases turn up, or two cycles pass without a
change in the board counts.
