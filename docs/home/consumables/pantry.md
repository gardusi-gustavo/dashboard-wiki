## 🧂 Seasoning

Seasoning is kitchen stock, not pantry food. Six 1 kg packs cost ~R$120 and
cover a long stay. Salt use is **25 g/week**; the other five spices use **5 g
each/week**. Buy the packs only for a 3+ month stay and donate/leave leftovers.

Full standard and batch amounts: `docs/reference/tools/seasoning.md`.

## 📦 Notes

- **Pizza is scheduled.** The base, ham, mozzarella, and eggs are weekly
  perishables, not off-plan items.
- **No lecithin.** Choline comes from beans, liver, and sunflower seeds.
- **No pasta in the baseline.** Pasta is optional and lives in the recipe index.
- Tomato sauce: check added sugar and sodium. Prefer plain crushed
  tomatoes/passata for the pizza.
- Seeds: 45 g/day × 7 = 315 g/week; 4.1 kg covers 3 months with a small buffer.
- Coffee: 500 g/month; buy monthly, keep one open.
- Nuts: 1/day; 0.45 kg covers about 91 nuts.
- Store cool and dry; keep bulk staples closed and away from light.
- Pantry stock is capped at 3 months. Buy three monthly packs; open one at a
  time.
