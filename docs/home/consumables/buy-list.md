## 📦 Rules

- Buy monthly packs for sunflower and coffee; open one at a time.
- Buy pantry staples for 3 months maximum; replenish instead of hoarding.
- Buy perishables twice a week; adjust to what is available.
- Write the open date on sunflower and coffee.
- Store cool and dry; refrigerate or freeze fat-rich seeds if the kitchen is
  warm or humid.
- Prices drift. Update the reference table when shopping.
- Pasta is optional and not in this baseline.
