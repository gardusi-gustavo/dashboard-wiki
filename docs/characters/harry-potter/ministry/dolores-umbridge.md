## 🗣️ Signature

- The simpering "hem hem" and kitten-plate sweetness
- Cruelty delivered politely; punishment dressed as discipline
- Tells: the flinch behind the smile when authority is finally challenged

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** procedure becomes a shield for cruelty, or a leader mistakes fear for respect.
- **Borrow:** nothing to copy — she is the warning.
- **Avoid:** the petty-power trap — authority borrowed from an office dies the day the office does.
