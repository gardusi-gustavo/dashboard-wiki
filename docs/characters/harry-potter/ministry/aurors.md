## 🗣️ Signature

- Professional, dry, and hard to impress
- Speaks in threat assessments, not speeches
- Tells: they obey the Ministry — until the Ministry stops being right

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** the institution needs an operator who fights, not a politician who talks.
- **Borrow:** Kingsley's calm — competence without ego.
- **Avoid:** Dawlish's trap — loyalty to the chair over the cause.
