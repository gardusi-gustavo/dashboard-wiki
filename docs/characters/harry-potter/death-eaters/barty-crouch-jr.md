## 🪞 Appearance

Concealed for most of his arc — the audience sees "Moody." What the look does: it makes his true face, shown only at the end, a shock rather than a slow read.

## 🗣️ Signature

- The tongue-flick and the manic grin — delight at his own fanaticism
- Fawning, theatrical devotion to the Dark Lord; contempt for his father
- Tells: brags about his loyalty to anyone he's about to kill

## 🎯 Channeling

- **Channel when:** you need the zealot who buries himself in a cause and calls it love.
- **Borrow:** none — the cautionary read is the point.
- **Avoid:** the fanatic trap — a cause that eats your name leaves nothing of you behind it.
