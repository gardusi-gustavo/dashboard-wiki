# 🧬 Archetypes — builds, resource loops, and min-max

**What this is:** a Sims/League read of people as **builds** — what resource loop keeps them running,
which cosmetic upkeep is a trap, and why accepting the starting stats is often the efficient move.
**Metaphor only, not a verdict on a person.** Pairs with [born.md](born.md) (spawn kit),
[balance.md](balance.md) (powers), and [gates.md](../fundamentals/gates.md) (rooms and flags).

---

## 🔁 Resource loops

Every build runs on something. The important split is **internal generation vs draining others**.

<!-- begin table -->
| Build | Loop | Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Internal** | Generates energy from craft, routine, exercise, sleep | Sustainable; the tank refills on its own |
| **Morgana** | Recharges by damaging or draining — spell vamp | Conflict is the resource; distance is the counter |
| **Cruella** | Status and validation from diminishing others | Needs an audience; the drain is the point |
| **High-maintenance** | Constant cosmetic upkeep and confirmation | Fragile when the mirror or the crowd goes away |
<!-- end table -->

Recognizing the kit is the move. You do not fix a spell-vamp build by giving it more mana; you
**space** it so its abilities cannot hit you.

---

## 💇 Cosmetic traps

Some builds spend most of their daily stamina on **masking the base stats**:

- Hair transplants, wigs, and constant mirror-checking are upkeep, not output.
- The high-upkeep build needs **validation** to keep the buff — public perception becomes a second job.
- The trap is not the choice; it is paying for the mask **instead of** the thing the mask is supposed
  to signal.

The efficient move is to compare **total cost**: money, time, attention, and the risk of the mask
failing. A cheaper build with more time for training often wins the long run.

---

## 🥋 Accept the build

The Saitama read: a bald fighter accepts the base stats and reallocates the upkeep into **diet,
training, and skill**. No wig, no mirror tax, no social-media confirmation loop.

- **Accept spawn** does not mean resign — it means stop pretending you rolled a different model.
- **Max EV on the actual car** is still the game ([born.md](born.md)).
- The recovered hours go into the gym, the craft, and the routines that actually compound.

A person who accepts the build can become **the strongest version of that build**; a person who
fights the character select screen spends the run in the menu.

---

## ⚖️ Min-max, not surrender

<!-- begin table -->
| Move | Pays | Costs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lean into the build** | Skill, identity, compounding | Less status from the generic ideal |
| **Patch the weak stat** | Covers a real gap | Time and money; may never be enough |
| **Ignore the meta** | Freedom from comparison | Social friction, fewer easy rooms |
<!-- end table -->

The point is not that one build is better. It is that **every build has an upkeep**, and the efficient
player knows which upkeep is buying output and which is buying applause.
